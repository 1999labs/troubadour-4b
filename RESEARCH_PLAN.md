# Troubadour: prospective experiment plan

Research question: Can post-training teach a model street smarts so that it can learn and apply unfamiliar social rules?

Operational question: Can post-training improve inference of unfamiliar synthetic social conventions from demonstrations? "Street smarts" is the project framing, not a claim of real-world social competence.

This is a controlled rule-induction study in social language, not a test of real-world cultural competence or human theory of mind. Prior work includes MetaICL (NAACL 2022), Symbol Tuning (EMNLP 2023), and work on semantic priors in in-context learning. Novelty is not claimed without a wider literature review.

Starting checkpoint: mlx-community/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-4bit, frozen revision recorded before use. Same quantization, prompt, and decoding are used before/after training. LoRA adapters are initialized fresh from this starting checkpoint.

We represent an encounter with five binary cues: recipient status relative to giver, public/private setting, accepting/declining a token, prior favor, visitor/local recipient. Synthetic A/B meanings are respect/breach. Both polarity assignments occur equally via paired complement worlds. No real cultural labels.

Rule grammar: constants; a single optionally negated cue; AND of 2 or 3 optionally negated cues; majority of 3 optionally negated cues; XOR of 2 or 3 cues, optionally complemented; a switch cue choosing between two optionally negated cues. The finite grammar and all input attributes are disclosed to the model. Different expressions computing the same truth table are deduplicated. A society is identified by its truth table, not its name.

The reference solver enumerates every rule consistent with observations. Answer A/B only when all survivors agree on the query; otherwise U. A hidden generating rule does not suffice to identify a label. Queries never duplicate observed full contexts. Tables and prose are alternative renderings of the same states.

Split by truth-table complement pairs. Training, development and final rule identities are disjoint. Two three-cue support combinations are reserved as structural transfer domains; development and final transfer rules are also disjoint. New random seeds alone are not called novel rules. Final data will be generated only after selection is frozen, from a committed split manifest and seed. Public release includes all splits after evaluation.

Pilot may change difficulty and size. Any change is recorded before the final test. Final data never selects checkpoints. Development may select among bounded learning rates, rank and answer-only/brief-reasoning recipes. Primary comparison: exact A/B/U generated answer at 8 demonstrations. Secondary: answerable accuracy, uncertainty recall/precision, balanced class accuracy, paired-world both-correct, structural transfer, paraphrase robustness, and 0/1/2/4/8 demonstration curves. Curves score each prefix against its own oracle; separately report determined coverage and results conditional on answerability. Do not equate increased uncertainty correctness with learning more conventions.

Controls: original Qwen under the same protocol; nearest-example/Hamming heuristic; constant-answer frequencies; reference upper bound. A budget-matched SFT control sees explicit rules rather than needing to infer them. Optional symbol rendering control checks whether results reflect social framing rather than abstract rule induction. Seeds and total tokens are logged. A generative answer test is primary; constrained-choice probabilities, if used for development speed, are diagnostic and clearly marked.

After pilot: freeze a meaningful effect threshold, sample size and selection rule. Tentative target +10 percentage points joint accuracy at 8 examples, positive paired confidence interval, meaningful improvement on answerable cases and structural transfer without a collapse in uncertainty handling. Use society-pair clustered bootstrap intervals; repeat selected recipe at least 3 training seeds when compute permits. If only one seed is feasible, explicitly label as exploratory and do not claim repeatability.

No RL in initial study. Evaluate low-cost SFT first. Record compute, energy/power interruptions, unsuccessful trials and limitations. Publishing a checkpoint must not imply it passed a success threshold if it did not.

## Pilot amendment 1 (before any training or final generation)
The 8-token free-generation pilot produced truncated explanations on some questions, confounding reasoning with format adherence. Primary evaluation will therefore select the highest-probability next token among A/B/U with identical constrained choice for base and adapted models. These are normalized choice probabilities, not calibrated confidence. A 256-token unconstrained-generation diagnostic remains separate; its formatting errors are not evidence of worse rule induction. Historical pilot outputs remain preserved.

## Pilot audit
The grammar has 612 unique truth tables (306 complement pairs). There are 165 training pairs, 40 development pairs, 51 final pairs, and 25 each development/final structural-transfer pairs. Initial 6,000 generated training rows were deduplicated by public prompt to 5,502. No duplicate prompts remain in development. Nearest-example heuristic: 62.5% development and 60.0% transfer. Final set remains ungenerated. Final primary set planned at 3 paired episodes per society pair: 306 IID and 150 transfer rows. Each set is class-balanced (A/B/U) by paired sampling and specified evidence strata; scores are not natural population prevalence estimates.

## Pilot amendment 2: chat-template audit (before valid training)
The cached tokenizer inserts an empty thinking block in assistant completions, but not in its generation prefix. It also ignores enable_thinking=False. Therefore the default supervised target included thinking markup while constrained evaluation expected a letter immediately. The first SFT trial was interrupted before its first checkpoint. We use an explicit simple ChatML template for training and evaluation; original cached files are never modified. For these single-turn inputs the generation prompt is identical to the original prefix, but supervised completions now start directly with A/B/U. Initial 48-example scores remain exploratory and are superseded by the full baseline. A token-prefix audit and batched/scalar scoring parity check are required. Failed trial logs remain available.

## Additional control, fixed before valid training
Fit a single uncertainty-logit bias to original Qwen's development choice probabilities (grid -8 to +8 in 0.25 increments; ties prefer bias closest to zero). Apply it unchanged on final data. Also report the same procedure for the trained candidate. This is a strong low-cost control for mere abstention-prior correction. An improvement over raw Qwen that disappears against this control is not claimed as evidence of improved rule induction. No A/B-specific bias is fitted, preserving complement symmetry.

Learning curves must not be interpreted using joint A/B/U accuracy alone: zero evidence correctly yields U for every query, giving trivial perfect uncertainty classification. On a fixed cohort of queries identifiable at eight observations, report the fraction correctly and justifiably resolved at each prefix, alongside oracle-resolvable coverage and unjustified certainty. Also show answerable-only accuracy and uncertainty recall. This separates learning local answers from recognizing ignorance.

The matched SFT control will use the same examples, targets, order, training steps and LoRA setup, but receive the correct answer explicitly in an appended answer key. Thus it practices reading the task and producing A/B/U without inferring a local convention. Its prompts are slightly longer, so this is equal-update/equal-example, not exactly equal-token compute. This replaces the proposed explicit-rule control, which would remove all U targets and confound label distribution. Such a control is informative about format/task exposure but does not isolate every possible mechanism.

## Hardware interruption and recovery
The corrected initial SFT run failed at update 85 with Metal's Impacting Interactivity error. The update-50 checkpoint was saved. A recovery pilot uses batch size 1, smaller allocator cache, an explicit memory limit, and checkpoints every 25 updates. It warm-starts from update50, with optimizer state reset and a new data-order seed (18); it is NOT an exact optimizer-state resume or a new independent training replicate. Record it as a distinct pilot recipe. Evaluate its saved checkpoint before deciding further training.

The failure matches the upstream macOS display/GPU context-switch issue documented by MLX maintainers at https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx/issues/3267. Recovery uses their process-local AGX_RELAX_CDM_CTXSTORE_TIMEOUT=1 workaround; no global OS settings are changed. Batch1 with accumulation2 preserves two examples per optimizer update. 300 microsteps add150 optimizer updates to the saved50, for200 retained updates total; discarded failed-run updates are separately logged as compute overhead.

## Targeted classification pilot (before any final data)
The update-50 generic SFT checkpoint predicts U on all48 pilot development questions (33.3%, below original Qwen on that subset). It is not a successful model. A new pilot starts from the original base, using balanced A/B/U training episodes with8 observations and LoRA cross-entropy over exactly the three evaluated answer tokens. The base architecture and inference path stay unchanged; no external classifier, hand-built solver features, or oracle outputs enter inference. This is supervised token classification, not general text SFT or RL. EOS/formatting tokens do not dominate its loss. Short-context performance becomes a transfer test rather than a trained curriculum. Keep both failed pilot artifacts and clearly document the changed objective and data selection. It is not a continuation of the warm-recovery weights.

## Query-dependence diagnostic (before candidate evaluation)
A query-blind majority-label heuristic scores48.33% on development (above raw Qwen's42.5%), so we add an opposite-query stress test. Within each world, two queries share exactly the same observations but have determinate opposite answers. A query-blind predictor cannot answer both correctly. Complement worlds remain paired for clustering. Constant rules and any world for which the sampler cannot construct such a pair are excluded and counted. This is a separate stress distribution, not a replacement of the main benchmark or a representative estimate of all societies. Main success criteria remain frozen; claims of learned rule use must discuss this diagnostic. Final contrast examples remain ungenerated until model selection is frozen.

## Longer, lower-rate pilot (before final data)
The 300-example choice pilot repeatedly predicts U in its development run. A new recipe starts from original Qwen, reduces the peak learning rate from1e-4 to5e-6, and averages8 examples per optimizer update (batch2, four microsteps). It processes6,000 examples over750 optimizer updates, with warmup and cosine decay; the2,850-example balanced training set is unchanged. This tests a longer, less disruptive optimization schedule. Development selects among saved checkpoints; final data remains untouched.

To make repeated passes practical, frozen lower28 transformer blocks can be computed once per exact input and cached without changing precision. Only prompt activations are stored; no target token, hidden rule or reference-solver feature enters them. The last8 blocks and original output embedding perform the same model computation as before. Cache keys bind the base revision, template, layer boundary and prompt tokens. Cached/full answer probabilities and adapter gradients must match before this optimization is used. Inference remains the complete Qwen model with its LoRA adapter; activation caching is optional and not part of the learned model.

## Prepared counterfactual practice recipe (not yet trained)
If the longer pilot still learns weak shortcuts, an additional supervised recipe keeps two A, two B and two U examples inside every optimizer update. Most groups contain exactly the same observations with three different queries whose correct labels are A/B/U, plus the complementary world. Thus a prediction based only on observation-label counts cannot fit the whole group. Twenty percent of groups are ordinary balanced examples, preserving exposure to cases with uniquely identified rules. All practice worlds come from the existing training allocation; no evaluation world or final question is used. The prepared dataset has3,690 examples in615 groups of six. It changes training example selection and batching together, so any gain cannot be attributed to a single mechanism without a further ablation. This is a candidate recipe, not a measured result.

The complementary-world both-correct metric is also reported on answerable pairs alone. The mixed metric can improve merely by always answering U on underdetermined pairs, so it must never be presented as sufficient evidence of learning a convention.

## Evaluation provenance hardening
Before final generation, evaluation metadata was extended to bind the exact renderer/template source and runtime package versions. The v3 metadata change does not change prompts, label probabilities, or the decision rule. Completed v2 development results remain historical comparisons made with the same unchanged prompt content. New output files are required for v3; an old run cannot silently append results after a renderer change. Final selection and final reports must carry the renderer hash.

## Threshold-independent diagnostics
Report conditional A/B accuracy on determinate questions (ignore U while choosing between A and B), and the AUC of the U-versus-best-A/B logit margin for identifying underdetermined questions. Both are invariant to a single global U-logit adjustment. These are diagnostic, not replacements for the frozen primary criteria. They help distinguish learning the local answer or recognizing missing evidence from changing how often the model abstains.

## Development checkpoint selection

For the longer low-rate run, full-development checkpoints are planned at 600, 1200, 2100 and 3000 microsteps. Earlier 60-question probes are diagnostic and do not replace the 240-question development set. If the longer recipe remains weak, the prepared counterfactual recipe may be evaluated as a separate candidate. Among fully evaluated seed 17 candidates, prefer candidates meeting the development analogues of the primary criteria; rank eligible candidates by joint A/B/U accuracy, then uncertainty recall, then earlier checkpoint. If no candidate meets those criteria, select the highest development accuracy by the same tie breakers and explicitly label the final release as a research checkpoint rather than a validated improvement. No reserved final examples select a checkpoint, recipe, threshold, or reporting metric.
